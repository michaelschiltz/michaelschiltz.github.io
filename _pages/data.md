* **Yokohama Specie Bank database, 1881–1945.** A comprehensive and annotated database of YSB midterm reports: several hundred thousand observations on deposits, note issue, inter-branch flow of funds, bills, and loans. [Figshare project](https://figshare.com/projects/Yokohama_Specie_Bank___/2173)
* **Research logbooks (_even-keel_).** Database architecture, inclusion/exclusion decisions, software choices, tests, and source assessments for the exchange-banking book project. [GitHub](https://github.com/michaelschiltz/even-keel)
* **HistorEE codebooks.** The data-and-documentation backbone of the HistorEE project, and the team-grade successor to _even-keel_: versioned, attributed datasets with machine-readable schemas, generated codebooks, controlled vocabularies, and a narrative decision log, so that every datum is traceable to a source and every coding decision to a person and a date. Code MIT, data CC BY 4.0. [GitHub](https://github.com/michaelschiltz/HistorEE_codebooks) · [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21341360) · [Figshare](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.32947250)
* **Working vault (Foam).** The idea-side companion to HistorEE and the book _Clearing and Settling the Realm_: a git-versioned [Foam](https://foambubble.github.io/foam) vault of atomic, cross-linked Markdown notes holding the arguments, distinctions, and objections from which chapters and grant prose are assembled, with maps of content, a controlled tag vocabulary, and an interactive knowledge graph. Where the codebooks hold the evidence, the vault holds the claims. Notes CC BY 4.0, code MIT. [GitHub](https://github.com/michaelschiltz/myfoamrepo) · [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21615890)
* **Capital Markets of the World.** A “smart catalog” for the Crédit Lyonnais collection of historical stock exchange price lists, 1850–2000, held at the Graduate Institute, Geneva. [GitHub](https://github.com/michaelschiltz/capital-markets-of-the-world) · [Report](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.4479545.v4)
* **OpenCourseWare Classical Japanese Grammar** (with David De Cooman, 2015; CC BY-NC). [Figshare](https://figshare.com/articles/OpenCourseWareClassicalJapaneseGrammar/1365602/3)
* **和蘭辞典 Japanese–Dutch dictionary.** Over 71,000 entries (CC BY-NC). [japansnederlandswoordenboek.org](http://japansnederlandswoordenboek.org/)
* **Interactive: the gold standard.** [Break the loop]({{ site.baseurl }}/gold_standard_interactive.html)
* **Zotero locales.** Dutch and Japanese localisations of Zotero (with Andreas Bovens and Ito Eiichi).
